# Module 1 Practice: Spot the Governance Issue

**Estimated time:** 10 minutes
**You'll need:** just the `customer-data.csv` file in this folder, no Python or GitHub account required. You can view it directly on screen or download it.

## Context

You've been handed a small extract of customer records, the kind of file a business team might drop into a shared drive without a second thought. Before anyone reports on it, sends it to a partner, or connects it to another system, someone in a governance role needs to look at it critically.

## Your task

Identify **two potential issues** in the dataset. For each one, write:

1. **The issue** — what looks wrong or uncertain
2. **Possible impact** — who or what gets hurt if this goes unaddressed
3. **The rule or definition needed** — what standard or policy would have prevented or caught this
4. **Who should decide** — which role (see Module 4) owns the call on how to handle it
5. **What you'd check before changing the data** — what you'd confirm first, rather than editing on sight

## A note on the negative value

Row C005 shows a negative `total_spent`. Resist the urge to flag this as an automatic error, a negative value could legitimately represent a refund. Treat it as a question to investigate (what does your source system actually record?), not a fact to correct. This is deliberate: good governance instincts ask *why* before they act.
